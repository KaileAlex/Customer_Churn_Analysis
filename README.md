📊 **Customer Churn Analysis using SQL & Excel**

I recently completed a customer churn analysis project using **MySQL and Microsoft Excel** to explore customer behaviour, identify churn patterns, and understand the potential revenue impact of customer loss.

Using SQL, I analyzed **3,000 customers** and found:

🔹 **44.5% overall churn rate**, with 1,336 customers having churned.

🔹 **Month-to-month customers had a 58.8% churn rate**, compared with 38.0% for one-year contracts and 14.5% for two-year contracts.

🔹 **Fiber Optic + Month-to-Month customers had a 73.4% churn rate**, highlighting a particularly high-churn customer segment.

🔹 **29.8% of churned customers cited “Price Too High”** as their recorded reason for leaving.

🔹 Churned customers accounted for approximately **49.7% of total monthly charges**, showing that churn can have a significant recurring-revenue impact.

### What I worked on

• Data exploration and cleaning
• SQL aggregation and filtering
• Churn-rate calculations
• Customer segmentation
• Identifying relationships between contract type, payment method, tenure, internet service and churn
• Excel analysis and reporting

This project helped me better understand how SQL can be used not just to retrieve data, but to turn raw customer records into **business insights that can support retention decisions**.
